Paperplane architecture

One upload, bounded processing, one grounded response.

1 · BrowserSelect a local PDF or image.
2 · Request guardAuthenticate, throttle, and validate input limits.
3 · FastAPIPOST /v2/parse owns the request.
4 · RenderPyMuPDF creates pages and native coordinates.
5 · Draft + verifyLuna drafts; Terra checks bounded ambiguity.
6 · ResponseGrounded Markdown and hierarchical JSON.

Uploads, results, jobs, and history are not retained by the application.
